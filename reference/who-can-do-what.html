Employer

Role: employer

An overseas company Marwa recruits for — a Japanese care home, a Gulf contractor. They see their own vacancies and the people Marwa has put forward, and nothing else.

11 built4 outstanding4 never
The one rule that shapes this whole role. An employer sees a candidate's complete professional file and CV, and never sees their phone number, email or home address. Interviews are arranged through Marwa. On the board this was modelled on, the candidate's contact details are literally the product — sold per unlocked number. Here the agency is the channel, and a worker's details staying inside it is part of what he gets for going through an agency at all.

What is left, across all three

Thirteen things, and one of them matters more than the other twelve put together.

Blocking

  • ○Outgoing mailConfirmation codes, stage notifications and alerts all go nowhere. A candidate who cannot confirm an email address cannot apply, so the portal has no working front door. Probably an unverified Brevo sender or an SMTP key where a v3 API key is wanted — I still have not seen the error text.

Waiting on a decision from Marwa

  • ○Document retention periodThe mechanism runs daily and is set to zero — keep everything forever. Nobody has chosen a number, and it should match the privacy notice.
  • ○Which employers may be named publiclyPer-employer switch exists. Until some are ticked, every card shows a lettermark rather than a logo.
  • ○The two drafted guidesPassport and medical. Both need Marwa's fees, timelines and partner centres.

  • ○Bangla translationThe cheapest doubling of reach available. Needs a native reviewer, not a machine.
